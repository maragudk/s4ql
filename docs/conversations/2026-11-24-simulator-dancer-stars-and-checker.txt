  **How it hears about sounds:** `sound.js` has a new hook, `watch(fn)`.
  `player(ctx, dest, played)` calls `played(kind, at)` only after `play` or
  `tick` has passed all its limits and decided to play. The page's player passes
  that on as `fn(kind, delay in ms)`, only while the audio context runs. It
  calls `fn(null)` when sound goes off, by the toggle or by a fault. A fault in
  `fn` is caught there and never reaches the sound. What is heard does not
  change. In `page.js`, `dancerHear` ignores sounds unless the page plays and
  reduced motion is off. `dance(ts)` runs in the page's animation frame and
  makes the figure stand at once when the page is paused, halted, under reduced
  motion, or when sound is off.

  **Turbo:** every pose holds at least 200 ms, so there are at most 5 poses a
  second. Only one move can wait for its turn. A higher rank takes that slot, or
  the newer sound if the ranks are equal, and a waiting move is dropped once it
  is 300 ms late. A move cuts into another only from a strictly higher rank, or
  after the current move has held all its poses. The figure stands again 450 ms
  after the last pose. In a playwright recording the shortest gap between two

  One premise is wrong, so it comes first. **A violation does not halt the page,
  and it does not use `#failure`.** `halt()` (`site/simulator/page.js:156-172`)
  fills `#failure` and is called only for failures of the engine or the page:
  lines 204, 219, 225, 229, 316 (S3 full), 867, 1112, 1123 and 1134. Nothing in
  `page.js` reads `violations`, `excused` or events of the kind `check`. A
  violation shows up as a line in the log, the sound `alarm` and the stick
  figure's panic move, and the page keeps playing.

  ## 1. What the checker is
  - It is the type `checker` in `internal/simulator/check.go`, built only with
  the tag `s4ql_browser` (line 1). `newChecker` is at :151 and is set up in
  `run.go:164`. It imports only `internal/format`. It sees only what the calls
  and the store see, nothing inside the core (README:746, 784).
  - **The six invariants** (constants at check.go:22-47; table at
  README:755-762):
    - `chain`: the manifest moves its head only to a commit object that is in
  the store and whose parent is the old head.
    - `acknowledged`: a write that ended `committed` is in the log, both when it
  is acknowledged and at the end.
    - `not_committed`: a write that ended `not_committed` or `down` is not in
  the log at the end. Also, every insert or delete of `barcodes` in the log
  comes from a call of the app or from the stored bucket.
    - `once`: no write is in the log twice, and the log deletes a row only where
  it holds it.
    - `state`: a served read's position is in the log, and the read gives its
  key's row exactly when the log up to that position has it. For a select of the
  visitor's SQL box, only the position is checked (README:773).
    - `fresh`: a read saw every write acknowledged before it started, and every
  write of unknown outcome that returned before it and is in the log at the end.
    - Only `fresh` can be excused: when the process's monotonic clock was
  stopped and its wall clock stopped or moved back (README:778; `p.bothClocks()`
  at check.go:587, 720).
  - **When it runs:**
    - `chain`: at every manifest write the store applies (`checker.manifest`,
  check.go:256, called from run.go:1525).
    - The commit object is recorded at every applied commit write (run.go:1536).
    - `acknowledged`: when a write ends `committed` (`writeEnded` :398, via
  run.go:691/693).
