# The workload of the generated checks

The generated checks run `stmttest.Sequence` by profile since 2026-11-21: `TestDifferential`, `TestConn_differential`, `TestSimulation`, and the planted bugs of the engine, and `TestGenerated` of the driver check. How the profiles and the streams work, and the steps to add an area, are in [`internal/sql/stmt/stmttest/README.md`](../../internal/sql/stmt/stmttest/README.md). The reasons are in the diary `2026-11-21-workload-profiles.md`.

## Decisions of the builder, to tell the lead

- **A seed names its profile, by a slot.** `stmttest.Slots` is 32, the ten profiles have the slots 0 to 9, and a seed of a slot that no profile has is of the mixed profile. A profile is appended to `stmttest.Profiles` and never moved. If more than 32 profiles are needed, `Slots` grows, and every count is recounted once.
- **The streams are by area and by statement.** A stream is a PCG seeded from the seed, the area's name, and the number of the statement, or the name of the table for a shape. The numbers that a statement draws do not depend on other statements; its text can, through what the statements before it stored.
- **The focused profiles aim.** `Profile.Aim` makes three statements in four name a table that the sequence created and did not drop, and `Profile.Faults` gives the statements beside the focus a quarter of their faults. Of the statements of the one sequence before, about half met no table. The mixed profile names its tables at random, as before, so the errors of a table that is not there stay as frequent there.
- **The sequence keeps the indexes that it created**, as far as its own statements tell, where it kept every create index that it drew, also one that SQLite refused. A WHERE that an index could serve is drawn for an index that is likely there.
- **Generator changes beyond the streams**, each to raise a kind that no profile reached three times: an update or a delete takes the key with a stored value one time in two (was three); a WHERE of an index has no pin and a bound on its first column one time in eight; with `Profile.Indexes` of 2 the key is pinned after the columns of an index one time in four, a create index leaves the key out three times in four, and a sequence has two or three names of indexes; `Profile.Columns` of 2 gives the profiles of the indexes and of the values on the left tables with a column beside the key; an update gives a real column one of the two zeros one time in four; the edges of text and blob hold a value of a byte more than the length limit; a drop table names an index that the sequence keeps one time in four of the aimed ones, and a create index one that it keeps one time in four.
- **The kinds that a test requires are a file that a builder writes** (`testdata/reached/<test>.kinds`), and the counts a file that the test writes (`.tsv`) with `S4QL_RECOUNT=1`. Only a required kind and an exact kind have rows. A required kind needs a row in one run at least, which means that a profile, or a cell or an environment of the simulation, reaches it three times; the least number is exactly a third, rounded down, and a run without any row fails.
- **Each kind of a `.kinds` file has a least number of its total over the runs, written by hand.** A recount never writes the file, so a floor falls only with a diff of it; a total below its number fails the check and the recount. In the simulation only the environments add, as their counts are the sums of those of their cells (`Reached.Totaled`). The numbers are the least numbers of `main` on 2026-11-21 for the same kinds: 393 of the 397 kinds of `TestDifferential`, 47 of 49 of `TestConn_differential`, all 82 of `TestSimulation`, as the sum over its three environments of 50 seeds, and 363 of 370 of `TestGenerated`. A kind that `main` did not require has a third of its total. No kind has a number below that of `main`. The review of the pull request found that a row lowered by hand together with its count, with the other rows of its kind deleted, passed the check, as a recount after a kind became rarer does; `TestReached_Check` has that case now.
- **`mixed` runs two thirds of the sequences that the test ran before the profiles**, since only it has every area on, and no kind of a focused profile counts where two areas meet that only `mixed` has on together: 200 of the 300 of `TestDifferential`, 100 of the 150 seeds of `TestSimulation` (34, 33, and 33 in the default, the idle, and the hammer environment), and 40 of the 60 of `TestConn_differential`, as before. `TestGenerated` of the driver check runs 128 of 250, where 167 were the aim: the time of its package may grow by a tenth at most, and under the race detector 144 took it 13 in 100 over the branch before. Its focused profiles run as many sequences as their rows allow: each reaches the least number of every row of its own of before, and each kind that only one profile reaches three times keeps its row. 26 rows of a kind that `changes`, `defaults`, `tables`, or `transactions` reached three to five times are gone, since the profile now reaches it once or twice; the totals of `TestGenerated.kinds` still hold each such kind. Two kinds count such a meeting: "insert that leaves out a column with a default that an index holds ok" and "value on the left on a table that a create table if not exists found", in `TestDifferential`, `TestConn_differential`, and `TestGenerated`. `TestSimulation` does not count them.
- **The sizes of the runs.** `TestDifferential`: 40 sequences of 48 statements of each profile, 80 of the profiles of the updates and deletes, the indexes, the clauses of slice 10, and the values on the left, and 200 of the mixed profile: 720 sequences, where there were 300. `TestConn_differential`: 20 sequences of 60 statements of each profile, and 40 of the mixed profile and of the profiles of the clauses, the defaults, and the values on the left: 280, where there were 60; two transactions in five are read-only, where one in three was. `TestSimulation`: 94, 69, and 101 seeds in the default, idle, and hammer environment, 264 in all, in cells of 6 to 34 seeds of a profile, where each ran 50; the default and the hammer environment run every profile and the idle one five, and each environment starts at seeds of its own, so no two run the same sequences. `TestDifferential_plantedBugs`: 30 sequences of the profile of each bug, where there were 60 of the one sequence. `TestGenerated` of the driver check: 13 to 56 sequences of 48 texts of each focused profile and 128 of the mixed profile (`generatedOf`), 411 in all, where there were 250; it compares 14,460 calls with SQLite, where it compared 8,487. It has no slow run, and checks its counts in every run.
- **`S4QL_ENGINE_SIM_SEEDS` sets the seeds of each cell** of `TestSimulation`, which then checks the least numbers alone if each cell runs as many seeds as its own at least, and no count otherwise. For `TestSimulation_plantedBugs` it is the limit of the seeds of the profile of each bug, as before.
- **The nightly workflow has a job `generated-engine`**: `TestDifferential` with `-sequences 400` and `TestSimulation` with 100 seeds of each cell. Each step checks the least numbers alone, and the second runs also when the first fails. The pull request changes a workflow file.
- **`TestSimulation` of the core fails for a minimum below 1.** The core's simulation does not use `stmttest.Sequence`: it has workloads of its own, and nothing else of it changed.

## Open

- **Open: the rows with a least number of 1.** Fourteen kinds of `TestDifferential` are reached three to five times by the profile that reaches them most, among them "WHERE with a column that the table does not have under IS NULL or IS NOT NULL, alone no such column" (4 times, by the profile of the values on the left). They hold, since the runs are a function of their seeds, and a change of the generator of their area can take one below three, which the recount then names. More sequences of the profile, or a statement of the generator that aims at the kind, raises one.
- **Open: the required kinds whose total is under 6**, which the review of the pull request left as they are, each with a row in one run: in `TestDifferential` "chosen an index with 2 pinned and a range by a value on the left" (3); in `TestConn_differential` the four kinds of the defaults and the values on the left in a transaction with a least number of 1 in `main` (3 to 5); in `TestGenerated` "read-only transaction: insert while rows are open" (4), "create table if not exists duplicate column name" (5), and four kinds of a value on the left: of `<=`, `>`, and `>=` that finds rows, and of `!=` with the wrong number of arguments (3 to 5). Higher odds in the profile that reaches each would move every row of that profile in all four tests, and its digest.
- **Open: two kinds that the driver check requires once, and not by profile.** "reached: value on the left of != finds rows" and "reached: WHERE with a column that the table does not have under IS NULL or IS NOT NULL, alone no such column" are not in `TestGenerated.kinds`, because no profile reaches them three times in its run. `TestGenerated` requires each once over all profiles, and `TestDifferential` of the engine requires both by profile. More sequences of the profiles of the values on the left and of the WHERE, or a statement of the generator that aims at the kind, gives each a row.
- **Open: an area that joins the mixed profile moves the rows of `mixed`.** Every statement of the mixed profile but those of the new area keeps its text, and the counts of `mixed` are recounted with those of the new profile. No row of another profile moves.
- **Open: the comments beside the planted bugs and in `internal/engine/README.md` give the shares of 200 seeds of 2026-11-21**; they are counted again when the generator of a profile changes, with `S4QL_ENGINE_SIM_SEEDS=200`.
- **Open: the thin kinds of slice 13 in `TestDifferential`.** Only the profile `order` reaches each of them three times or more, after the review of 2026-11-21 aimed the profile at the keys of its indexes: "LIMIT that cuts a group of rows that tie" 4 times, "delivered select whose rows that tie came in the order of the key of its access" 14 times, which fell from 31 when mixed directions took most ORDER BY of a key, and "sorted select whose rows that tie came in the order of the primary key" and its reverse 8 and 14 times. Their least numbers in `TestDifferential.kinds` are 1, 4, 2, and 4. More sequences of the profile raise them. Higher odds in `Sequence.order` or `Sequence.orderColumns` raise them too, and then the rows of `order` and of `mixed` are recounted in all four tests, with both digests.
