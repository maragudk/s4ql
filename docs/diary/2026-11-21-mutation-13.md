# Diary: Mutation testing of slice 13

Slice 13 (`order by` and `limit` on `select`, #119, #122, #124, and the generated checks of #125) is built and merged, and no round of the mutation tester `internal/sql/mutation` has run over its code. This task runs it over the lines that those pull requests wrote, kills every survivor with a test of what the code promises or lists it in `equivalent.txt` with a reason, and fixes any fault it shows. The branch is `mutation-13`, from `origin/main` at `29de57a`.

## Step 1: The selection, the first runs, and the pass

**Author:** mutation-13 builder

### Prompt Context

**Verbatim prompt:** (excerpt from the lead's brief) "Mutation testing of slice 13 of `docs/roadmap.md`, `order by` and `limit` on `select`, which is built and has its generated checks but no mutation testing [...] Scope: the lines those three pull requests wrote in non-test code, as they stand on `main` now. Lines of the planted bugs in `internal/engine/bugs.go` and their hooks are mutated like the earlier rounds did; variants of a planted bug are listed as equivalent the way `equivalent.txt` already does it. Result wanted: every mutant in scope is killed by a test, or listed in `equivalent.txt` with a reason a reader can check [...] or removed with code that nothing needs."
**Interpretation:** Select the mutants on the lines that #119, #122, and #124 wrote, by `git blame` as the last round did, and with them the lines of the planted bugs of slice 13 and their hooks, which #125 wrote; run each package on a copy of the tree under `/Volumes/990/s4ql/mutation-13/`; classify every survivor.
**Inferred intent:** The same trust in slice 13 as in the slices before it.

### What I did

- The scripts are in `/Volumes/990/s4ql/mutation-13/scripts/`: `pick.py` maps every commit of each pull request (the range `M^1..M^2` of its merge `M`, and `M`) to its number, blames every file of the `-list` of the tool at `HEAD`, and keeps the mutants on a line that one of those commits wrote; `regex.py` turns them into a `-run` expression of `file:line` per package; `copy.sh` copies the worktree to a tree there; `run.sh` runs the tool with `GOCACHE`, `TMPDIR`, and `-gocache` on that disk; `wait.sh` waits under the three minutes after which the harness moves a command to the background, and prints the new survivors.
- `/Volumes/990/s4ql/mutation-13/emit/` is a copy of the tool's files with a `main` of its own that writes the file of one mutant by its key, and `scripts/mut.sh` runs a test on that mutant through `go test -overlay`, so a new test is checked against its mutant in seconds, on the worktree, while the runs read the copy.
- `-list` over `stmt`, the engine, the core, `internal/sqlite`, and the root gave 17,317 mutants; the selection kept 1,299: `stmt` 209 (#119), the engine 759 (569 of #122 and 190 of #125: `bugs.go` and the hooks of the planted bugs in `access.go` and `operator.go`), the core 274 (#122 and #124), `internal/sqlite` 57 (#124, without `sqlite_browser.go`, which the build of the tests leaves out). The driver at the root has no line of these pull requests: #122 changed only its tests.
- The run of `stmt` (2 workers) ended: 209 mutants, 25 not compiled, 179 killed, 5 survived. `TestCheck` now refuses `limit 0X5` as a hex literal, and a name in brackets after `"[v]"` in a SET and in the columns of an index, which dequote to the same text; the other two, the accept of the column of an ORDER BY and of the LIMIT, are equivalent.
- In the core, `ended` lost its `err != nil`, which `errors.Is` makes for it, and `TestEnded` in `/internal/core/errors_internal_test.go` pins what it reports.

### Why

The planted bugs of slice 13 came with its generated checks in #125, not with the three pull requests of the brief; the brief names them, so their lines are in.

### What worked

- The overlay checker killed each mutant that a new test was written for on the first try.

### What didn't work

- A foreground `sleep 170` was refused: `Blocked: sleep 170 followed by: ... To wait for a condition, use Monitor with an until-loop`. The waits are an `until` loop on the clock now, in `wait.sh`.
- A shell command that wrote several files with heredocs and `cp` from the worktree was refused as "too complex to verify that it stays inside the worktree"; the files of the emitter were written one by one.

### What I learned

- The case of `"[v]"` and `[v]` is what tells the quoting check apart from the check of a name named twice: `dequote` turns `"[v]"` into `[v]`, and leaves `[v]` as it is, so a bare name before it does not.

### What was tricky

- The load of the machine was 14 before the runs and 25 to 27 with them, from other agents too; the runs are slow for it.

### What warrants review

- The line drawn around #125: only its non-test lines in the engine, which are the planted bugs and their hooks.

### Future work

- The survivors of the engine, the core, and `internal/sqlite`.

## Step 2: The survivors of the engine, the core, and the binding, a fault, and a stop for the disk

**Author:** mutation-13 builder

### Prompt Context

**Verbatim prompt:** (from the brief) "If a survivor shows a real fault in s4ql, fix it with a test that pins it and say so; if the fix is large or changes an agreed rule, stop and report. [...] Run `df -h / /Volumes/990` before you start and after the first shard, and stop if the main disk has under 20 GB free (it has 37 GB now)."
**Interpretation:** Classify each survivor as it comes, with a test checked against its mutant at once, fix a fault that is small, and stop the runs if the main disk falls under 20 GB.
**Inferred intent:** Every mutant of slice 13 accounted for, without filling the disk that every agent shares.

### What I did

- The engine's run ended: 759 mutants, 171 not compiled, 502 killed, 3 timed out, 83 survived. 72 are equivalent, 66 of them variants of the planted bugs `turnMixed`, `skipGap`, `ascendLast`, `limitFirst`, and `lastIndexWins`, and three were in `equivalent.txt` already from slice 9; 10 are killed by `TestLimit`, `TestLimitCount`, and new cases of `TestBuild` and `TestFilter`; one was a guard that nothing needs. The core's ended: 274, 95 not compiled, 142 killed, 37 survived, of which 18 are killed by new tests, 11 were in code that went, and 8 are equivalent. The binding's run had 40 of its 57 mutants when it stopped: 12 survived, 5 killed by new tests, 2 in code that went, 5 equivalent.
- A survivor showed a fault: `conn.scanIndex len(scan.Order) > 0 => >= 0` survived, and asking why led to a scan by a key of more than 63 columns, which SQLite sorts in its first step under the connection's limit. A throwaway test with 64 REAL key columns that hold whole numbers and a text that fills the row to a limit of 3,000 gave `sqlite: string or blob too big (code 18)`, and in the write transaction `the transaction can only be rolled back, after: sqlite: string or blob too big (code 18)`. `conn.scanIndex` now passes `sorting` for such a scan too; the new subtest of `TestScanIndex` fails on `main` with that error and passes with the fix.
- The new sections of `/internal/sql/mutation/equivalent.txt` come from `/Volumes/990/s4ql/mutation-13/scripts/eq.py`, which checks every key against a `-list` of the final code. `listedPkgs` in `/internal/sql/mutation/main.go` has `internal/sqlite` now, since `TestEquivalent` refused its keys with `no such mutant`.
- The main disk had 5.4 GB free at the copy of the tree for the reruns, under the 20 GB of the brief, though the runs wrote only to `/Volumes/990` (`GOCACHE`, `TMPDIR`, and `-gocache` there, and the default Go cache of the machine is there too). I stopped the run of the binding and started no rerun.

### Why

The brief's limit on the disk is a hard stop; the runs of the binding and the reruns would build and run every test package many times more.

### What worked

- Checking each new test against its mutant with `scripts/verify.sh`, which now tells a build failure from a kill: the first `TestLimit` used `*values` as a source, which does not compile (`*values does not implement source (missing method columns)`), and the first version of the checker had counted that as a kill.

### What didn't work

- `TestEquivalent` failed twice: `the key "internal/engine/operator.go build.access condition: err != nil => false" is listed twice`, since slice 9 had listed two of the engine's survivors and one of the telemetry already, and `no such mutant: internal/sqlite/sqlite.go Stmt.StepContext ...` before `listedPkgs` named the binding.
- A test of "no direction in the event of a query that fails before its access" passed on its mutant: the engine records the direction only after a query is built, so the mutant `r.access != "" => true #2` of `report.end` is equivalent, and the test went again.
- A command that appended notes with a heredoc and then ran the checker in the worktree was refused as too complex, and its notes were lost; the decisions were rebuilt from the list of survivors.

### What I learned

- The cost of a planted bug's variant: each one that survives runs the whole simulation, 10 minutes under that load, so `bugs.go`, with 107 of the engine's 759 mutants, took most of its 4 hours.

### What was tricky

- The count of the unread rows of a sort is a second statement whose cost no row shows; `ctxtest.Context.Calls` counts the looks of SQLite's progress handler, so a count by the wrong index, or a count that should not run, shows as looks after the last row.

### What warrants review

- The fix in `conn.scanIndex` and its test, and the removal of the error checks of `SetLengthLimit` in `conn.step`.
- The reasons in the sections of slice 13 of `equivalent.txt`, above all the first step of a scan that does not sort under the largest limit, which rests on the cache holding no value longer than the limit of its connection.

### Future work

- The last 17 mutants of `internal/sqlite` (`-run` of `/Volumes/990/s4ql/mutation-13/re_sqlite.txt`, which skips those with a result in `out-sqlite-1`), the reruns on the final code (`scripts/rerun.py`), `make test`, `make lint`, the self-review, and the pull request.
