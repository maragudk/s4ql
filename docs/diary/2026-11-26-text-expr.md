# Diary: Text in expressions, the evaluator

The second step of the piece of work "text in expressions": the evaluator in `/internal/expr` computes `||`, `lower`, and `hex`, which the statement pass reads since pull request #172. The engine keeps refusing every statement with text until its own step. The work is on the branch `text-expr`, from `135b966c` on `main`.

## Step 1: SQLite's rules, the design, and the code

**Author:** text-expr builder

### Prompt Context

**Verbatim prompt:** (excerpt from the lead's brief) "Your ONE task is the second step of the piece **text in expressions**: the EVALUATOR, `internal/expr`, for the operator `||` and the functions `lower` and `hex`. [...] `internal/expr` checks and computes expressions with `||`, `lower`, and `hex`, alone and mixed with arithmetic where the types allow, over literals, parameters, and columns [...] SQLite refuses a result longer than its length limit ("string or blob too big"); the engine runs SQLite with a length limit that tests lower. Find how the arithmetic step treats limits, and give SQLite's answer or an evaluation error of a new kind for a result over the limit [...] Text results must allocate; keep it to what is needed"
**Interpretation:** Give the evaluator a type check by place, the three operations byte for byte as SQLite computes them, SQLite's length limit, and a new kind of evaluation error; keep arithmetic as it is and free of allocations.
**Inferred intent:** The middle of goqite's id, `'m_' || lower(hex(randomblob(16)))`, computed exactly, so that the engine step has only to wire it in.

### What I did

Read `/AGENTS.md`, `/docs/todo/text-functions.md`, `/docs/todo/arithmetic.md`, the diary of the statement pass, and all of `/internal/expr`. Read `OP_Concat`, `contextMalloc`, `lowerFunc`, and `hexFunc` in `/internal/sqlite/c/sqlite3.c`, and measured with the shell (3.54.1, `/opt/homebrew/opt/sqlite/bin/sqlite3`) under `.limit length 30`, with long values in a table so that the statements stayed short.

Rewrote `/internal/expr/expr.go` around a `place` of each node (the root, arithmetic, `||`, `lower`, `hex`), which decides what a column at `Check` and an argument at `Bind` may be; `/internal/expr/value.go` holds text and blobs in a `string`; `/internal/expr/errors.go` has `ErrTooLong` and a field `Function` on `*Error`. `Program.Bind` takes the length limit. Outside the package: `bindArithmetic` in `/internal/engine/expression.go` passes `math.MaxInt`, with a comment, since the engine refuses text before it binds.

### Why

The pass guarantees that a tree has no text under arithmetic and no arithmetic under text, so a tree of the evaluator is either; the refusals left to it are those that only the types of the columns and the arguments show. A place per node makes each refusal one rule, and its message names the place.

### What worked

The facts of SQLite, each checked in the shell and later pinned through the binding:

- `lower(s)` fails for `length(s)` equal to the limit, and not for one byte less; `s || t` fails only above the limit; `hex(s)` fails for 15 bytes under a limit of 30 and not for 14. SQLite allocates one byte more for `lower` and `hex`, for the NUL, and checks that allocation against the limit (`contextMalloc`), while `OP_Concat` checks the length itself.
- `hex(NULL)` is `''`: `sqlite3_value_blob` gives a null pointer of length 0, and `contextMalloc(1)` succeeds. `lower(NULL)` is NULL, since `lowerFunc` returns without a result.
- A column of text longer than the limit fails when it is read, and a statement longer than the limit fails when it is prepared ("Parse error ... string or blob too big" for every line of more than 30 bytes under `.limit length 30`).

### What didn't work

The first measurement used `printf('%.*c', 29, 'a')` inline, and every statement failed with `Parse error near line 2: string or blob too big (18)`, because the statement itself was longer than the limit of 30. Putting the long values in a table first, before `.limit`, answered the questions.

The harness refused two heredocs: "This agent is isolated in the worktree ..., but this command is too complex to verify that it stays inside the worktree." Writing the Python and Go to files first and running them as plain commands worked.

### What I learned

SQLite's length limit also bounds the text of a statement, so the oracle of the tests must keep its own query under the limit: the generator raises a case's limit to the length of the expression plus 200.

### What was tricky

A BLOB value in a `string` makes `Value` comparable with `==`, which every test relies on, but copying a blob from a row allocates. `hex` reads the bytes of a column or an argument where they are instead, so only a BLOB column alone copies, and the engine never asks for that.

### What warrants review

`Bound.hex` in `/internal/expr/expr.go`, which reads a leaf's bytes without a `Value` and checks the type of a column there; and the three checks of the limit, `+1` for `lower` and `2n+1` for `hex`.

### Future work

The engine step passes the database's limit to `Bind`, gives SQLite's `string or blob too big` for `ErrTooLong`, and checks the length of a literal of text; see "What the engine step needs to know" in `/docs/todo/text-functions.md`.

## Step 2: The differential test and the fuzz test of text

**Author:** text-expr builder

### Prompt Context

**Verbatim prompt:** (excerpt from the lead's brief) "The differential test and `FuzzExpr` extended to the new kinds, comparing with SQLite bit for bit, with generated expressions over text, blobs, nulls, mixed with arithmetic, nested to the depth limit, with text that is not ASCII, empty text, long text near the limit"
**Interpretation:** A generator of trees of text, a reference that computes them apart from the evaluator, an oracle that runs SQLite under the limit of the case, and the required kinds.
**Inferred intent:** The same trust in text as in arithmetic: every answer compared with SQLite's.

### What I did

Added `t TEXT` and `y BLOB` to the schema of the tests, a length limit to `exprCase` and `oracle.value` (which returns text, blobs, and `errTooBig` for SQLITE_TOOBIG), `reference.text`, the refusals of every place in `refusalOf`, and the text generator in `/internal/expr/generator_test.go`: `textCase`, `textOperator`, `deepTree`, `edgeOfLimit`, `lengthOf`, `fillText`, `plantNumber`, `writeText`, and `textLeaf`. `FuzzExpr` takes a limit and encodes a text or a blob by its length. Pinned the facts in `TestBound_Eval_text`, `TestCheck`, `TestProgram_Type`, `TestValue`, `TestError`, and the allocations. Recounted `/internal/expr/testdata/reached/`.

### Why

The arithmetic check is the model; text needs the same independence from the evaluator, and its edges are lengths, which only long values reach.

### What worked

The evaluator gave SQLite's answer on every generated case from the first run; every failure along the way was the generator's.

### What didn't work

- The first run gave hundreds of "not a case: s4ql: not supported: parentheses (at offset 21)": the generator put optional parentheses around a concatenation that was the argument of a call, which the pass refuses. Only an operand of `||` may have them now.
- Then "not a case: ... parentheses deeper than 100" for the deep chains, whose optional left parentheses piled up; a deep tree has none now.
- `TestDifferential` passed but the tallies showed 27,310 "|| of two empty texts" and 34,922 "|| with null". Two causes: `lengthOf` split a length at random down a chain of 900 operators, so all leaves past the first few got length 0; and one NULL leaf in a chain made every operation above it NULL. Deep trees now take no edge of the limit and no NULL.
- `TestBound_Eval` and `TestCheck` failed with "Expected "3.5", but got "NULL"" after the schema grew: the row of the tests had six values, so the oracle bound the arguments to the columns `t` and `y`. `row` gives eight now.

### What I learned

gofmt turns `''` in a doc comment into a typographic quote; the comments say "the empty text" instead.

### What was tricky

The oracle numbers a plain `?` of the expression after the eight of the row, so a case's row must have exactly as many values as the schema.

### What warrants review

`refusalOf` in `/internal/expr/differential_test.go`, which states the rule of the places apart from the evaluator, and the floors in `TestDifferential.kinds`, a third of the totals of 2026-11-26.

### Future work

None beyond what step 3 records.
